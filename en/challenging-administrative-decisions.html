Challenging Administrative Decisions

Assessment of public authority decisions, their effects and the available administrative and court remedies.

Discuss your matter

A refusal, revocation or other administrative decision may affect a project, contract or personal situation. The starting point is to establish what was decided, the reasons given and when it was communicated. We review the administrative file and prepare a response suited to the client’s position.

How we can help

  • Review of decisions and notifications
  • Access to and organisation of the administrative file
  • Assessment of grounds for challenge
  • Administrative complaints and appeals
  • Challenges before administrative courts
  • Assessment of interim protection where needed

Understanding the decision and the file

An initial review usually requires the full decision, its notification and evidence of receipt, together with the original application and earlier correspondence. A proposed decision is not the same as a final administrative act. We identify the procedural stage and any missing material before advising on the next step.

Choosing an appropriate remedy

The response may involve submissions within the procedure, an administrative complaint or appeal, or court proceedings. The appropriate route depends on the act, the competent authority, the intended outcome and the applicable requirements. Taking an administrative step should not be assumed to preserve every court deadline.

Deadlines and practical effects

Time limits require an individual assessment of the decision and its communication. If implementation could have significant consequences, we assess the need for interim relief and whether its requirements are met. Bringing a challenge should not be confused with an automatic suspension of the decision’s effects.
