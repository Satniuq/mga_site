Civil Liability and Compensation in Portugal

Assessment of loss, compensation claims and defence against civil liability allegations.

Discuss your matter

Loss can raise questions about who is responsible, which damages may be claimed and how to establish them. We assess compensation claims and defend parties facing allegations of contractual or non-contractual liability. Our work begins with reconstructing the events and identifying the available evidence.

How we can help

  • Assessment of facts and the basis of liability
  • Organisation of evidence of loss and causation
  • Review of compensation claims and amounts
  • Preparation of demands and responses
  • Negotiation with responsible parties and insurers
  • Representation in compensation proceedings

From events to legal responsibility

The existence of loss does not itself establish who must compensate it. We consider the conduct, applicable legal framework and connection between the events and the loss. Contracts, correspondence, reports and details of those involved help identify questions requiring evidence or technical assessment.

Documenting the loss

Expenses, lost income and other losses need to be organised with supporting records. Assessment may also involve non-financial harm and specialist evidence. The amount claimed should be grounded in the individual case, not a universal tariff or the claimant’s expectations alone.

Making or responding to a claim

We prepare claims and responses and assess opportunities for negotiation. Where insurance is involved, we review the relevant policy documents and communications. If court proceedings are necessary, the approach considers evidence, parties and applicable time limits, without guaranteeing that a particular amount will be awarded or recovered.
