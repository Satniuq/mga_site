Corporate and Commercial Law

Discuss your matter

We advise companies, business owners and investors on company formation, internal organisation, shareholder relationships and commercial contracts. Our work covers the start of a business, its development and periods of change or dispute.

How we can help

  • Company formation and reorganisation
  • Transfers of shares and changes in ownership
  • Articles of association and shareholders’ agreements
  • Corporate resolutions and governance
  • Commercial contracts
  • Prevention and resolution of shareholder disputes

Companies and shareholders

We advise on the rules governing a company, its management, decision-making and transfers of ownership. We assess the legal implications of changes to the business structure.

Commercial contracts

We draft, review and assist with the negotiation of contracts with customers, suppliers, distributors, partners and investors. Contractual terms should reflect the transaction and the company’s objectives.
