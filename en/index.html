MG&A

A law firm built on experience, method and integrated legal support.

Miguel, Goes & Associados is a mid-sized law firm founded in 1998, providing legal advice in Portugal in matters of Public Law and Private Law.

The firm's practice covers relations between citizens, companies and the State, as well as private, asset-related, family, corporate, employment and procedural matters.

Client support is approached through two complementary dimensions: prevention of disputes and resolution of disputes, with an integrated reading of each concrete situation.

Public Law

Relations with public authorities, procurement and urban planning.

Legal advice before public entities requires attention to administrative acts, deadlines, formal requirements and the way each public decision may affect the client's activity.
