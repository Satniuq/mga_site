Privacy Policy

Information on data processed through the Miguel, Goes & Associados website and contact form, service providers and your data protection rights.

Updated on 26 October 2026

Data controller

Miguel, Goes & Associados - Sociedade de Advogados, SP, RL is responsible for personal data received through enquiries. Contact us at advogados@mga.com.pt, by telephone on +351 21 319 3720, or by post at Avenida Miguel Bombarda, 147 - 1.º Dto., 1050-164 Lisbon, Portugal.

Data and purposes

The contact form collects the name, email address, subject and message you provide. We use this information to assess and respond to the enquiry and, where relevant, prepare a possible engagement.

An enquiry about services may be processed to take pre-contractual steps at your request. Other communications are processed to respond to the enquiry, taking account of the legitimate interests involved. Legal and professional obligations may require additional processing.

Service providers and external services

The contact form uses Formspree to receive and forward messages. The hosting provider may process technical access information to operate and secure the website.

The Google map is loaded only when you request it. Opening the map or an external link connects you to the relevant service, which has its own terms and privacy policy.

Formspree states that it uses infrastructure in the United States and may process data there. International transfers are subject to the GDPR and applicable safeguards. Provider information is available through the links below.

Retention

Enquiries and related data are kept for as long as needed to handle and follow up the request. The period depends on the nature of the enquiry, any professional relationship and applicable legal or professional retention requirements.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Send requests concerning your rights to advogados@mga.com.pt. You may also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD.

Cookies and browsing

This version of the website does not use its own advertising or statistical measurement cookies. Images, fonts and icons are served by the website itself. External services opened at your request may use their own browsing technologies.

Changes

This policy will be updated when there are relevant changes to processing or service providers. The version date is shown on this page.
