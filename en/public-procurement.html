Public Procurement

Advice on public tenders, bids, exclusion and award decisions, and the performance of public contracts.

On this page
  • How we can help
  • Preparing and submitting a bid
  • Decisions and challenges
  • Contract performance
  • People in this practice
Discuss your matter

We advise on public procurement procedures under the Portuguese Public Contracts Code. Both the substance and the formal requirements of a bid matter: a procedural omission may lead to the exclusion of an otherwise competitive offer.

How we can help

  • Review of tender documents and specifications
  • Requirements for applications and bids
  • Requests for clarification and communications
  • Exclusion and award decisions
  • Pre-contractual litigation
  • Public contract performance and disputes

Preparing and submitting a bid

Legal preparation supports the technical work required for a tender. We review the procurement documents, supporting information and grounds for potential exclusion, and help prepare the bidder’s legal position.

Decisions and challenges

Questions may arise before a contract is signed, including admission, scoring and award decisions. We assess the decision, available remedies and applicable deadlines.

Contract performance

We advise on contractual interpretation, compliance, changes and disputes arising during the performance of public contracts.
